A user-friendly Stage5 of Gentoo Linux.
Bentoo is a source-based Linux desktop that extends Gentoo's staged-install philosophy to its natural conclusion: Stage5 — a pre-compiled, pre-configured, daily-usable system. It keeps the power of Portage and the rigor of source builds, while shipping you a complete desktop on day one.
