Rotator behaviour

Visiting /coming-soon/ picks a random variant, stores it in localStorage.bentoo-variant with a 24h expiry, and redirects. Subsequent visits within the window stick to the same variant.

Force a specific variant for preview with ?v=v3i — this is a one-shot override that doesn't persist.
